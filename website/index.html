Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A clean, Spotify-style player for the music you already love. It runs on your own devices and works without an account.

YouTube playlists

Paste any public playlist link and every track downloads as a 192 kbps MP3, with titles and cover art.

Spotify playlists

OfflineBeats finds each Spotify track on YouTube Music and saves it for you. You don't need a Spotify account or Premium.

Your local library

Point it at any folder of MP3, FLAC, M4A, WAV or OGG files and they're ready to play in seconds. Your original files are never moved or changed.

Truly offline

Once a song is downloaded it's a file on your device, so it plays on a plane, on the subway or anywhere else with no signal, for as long as you keep it. There's a live audio visualizer, plus shuffle and repeat.

Native Android app

The Android app downloads straight to your phone and has lock-screen controls, background playback and drag-and-drop playlist ordering.

Phone access over WiFi

Scan a QR code in the desktop app and your phone streams your whole laptop library on the same WiFi.

Updates itself

The app checks for new versions when it starts and tells you when one is ready, so you don't have to come back to this site.

Private by design

There are no accounts, no tracking and no cloud. Your library and listening stay on your own devices.

How it works

From link to offline in three steps

Install OfflineBeats

Download the app for your Mac, Windows PC or Android phone below. Everything it needs comes in the installer, so there's nothing else to set up.

Paste a playlist link

Click + and paste a link like youtube.com/playlist?list=… or open.spotify.com/playlist/…. A single song works too.

Press play, anywhere

Tracks appear as they finish downloading. After that you can turn off WiFi and your music keeps playing.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is OfflineBeats really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. It's open source under the MIT license.

Do I need a Spotify or YouTube account?

No. Public playlists work without signing in to anything. For Spotify playlists, OfflineBeats only reads the track list and then downloads each song from YouTube Music.

Where are my songs stored?

On a Mac or PC, downloads go to Music/OfflineBeats in your home folder as normal MP3 files you can use anywhere. On Android, they're saved inside the app's own storage on your phone.

Why does my computer warn me when I open it?

OfflineBeats is an independent project and isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet. The source code is public on GitHub, and every installer is built there automatically by GitHub Actions. The install steps above show how to open it.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. In the meantime, use Open on Mobile in the desktop app to stream your library to Safari over your home WiFi.

Is downloading music legal?

OfflineBeats is a tool for personal, offline listening. Please only download content you have the right to, and respect the terms of the services you use and the artists who make the music.
